# Spotify playlists

Press `S` and pick a playlist for the scene, or paste a link. Each scene keeps its own. It plays through [Spotifast](https://spotifast.rocks), which needs Spotify Premium. Murmur starts it in the tray if it isn't running.

## The lock means private

Spotify gives other programs no private-session switch. Murmur gets the same result by playing on Spotifast's built-in player, whose plays Spotify doesn't record: nothing lands in your history or recommendations.

- If that player isn't set up, Murmur refuses to play instead of playing somewhere Spotify records.
- Click the lock to turn private off.
- Private plays aren't counted at all, so they don't count for the artists either.

## What it changes in Spotifast, and puts back

Murmur only runs Spotifast's documented commands and has no Spotify sign-in of its own.

- It moves playback to the built-in player, turns shuffle on and repeats the playlist.
- The balance slider and the fades set Spotify's volume.
- When you leave the scene, switch the stream off or close Murmur, it pauses and puts back the volume, shuffle, repeat and device.
- Your playlists are listed from the file Spotifast keeps on your PC. If it can't be read, the list is empty and a pasted link still works.

Murmur is not affiliated with Spotifast or Spotify.
