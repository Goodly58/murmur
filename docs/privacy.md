# Privacy

No accounts, analytics or telemetry. Your mix, timers, presets and study history stay on your PC, in `%APPDATA%\Murmur`.

Murmur goes online only for these:

| Who | What for |
|---|---|
| [YouTube](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Which live streams are on, the one you play, and its thumbnail |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | mpv and yt-dlp when you click **Set up streaming**, then a daily check for a newer yt-dlp |
| [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/) | The name and cover of a playlist you pasted as a link, and the cover of the song that plays. No sign-in |

Those services see what any web request shows them, such as your IP address.

Spotify playback itself goes through Spotifast on your PC, under your own account. Murmur reads your playlist names from Spotifast's files and sends them nowhere.
